## What is not done, or not as strong as it sounds

- **Third-party part data is unverified.** Part numbers, I2C addresses, supply ranges, sizes and costs for sensors, regulators and modules come from memory and are marked `unverified` in every kit, with an open question. Silicon Labs boards and links were checked on silabs.com (SiWx917 dev kit, xG24-DK2601B, xG27-DK2602A, SLTB010A); only the DK2605A has a confirmed price ($40).
- **EFR32 dev-kit pad mapping is a placeholder.** The BLE and Thread kits reuse the BRD2605A pad numbering and say so; map them to the real expansion headers before building.
- **Nothing in a reference kit has been built.** Their wiring pages say so on a banner; energy figures, battery lives and power budgets are estimates with their assumptions written next to them.
- **Solar is computed**, never measured; "yesterday" in the energy tile stays "no data yet" until the backend has run across a midnight; ETP limits (pH 6.5–8.5, 25 NTU) are example values.
- **Rooftop solar** shows on the warehouse and factory roofs only in the monitor's overview (those halls stay open on `/twin`); on the new buildings when the roof is closed.
- **Raw material docks** are graph nodes only; the movement engine does not assign trucks to them yet. Line 2 is data and 3D plus two machine monitors; OEE and "units today" stay line 1.
- The weighbridge has no dwell time yet; trucks drive over it.
- The daily report PDF is built from tested numbers but I did not open a downloaded file in a viewer.
- The Interiors toggle was checked on the FAC-01 view, not building by building; per-building screenshots were not saved.
- Chrome pauses React effects in background tabs, so the stats hook only appears once the tab has been in front.
- Fire panel and gas nodes **advise only**; they are not certified systems and the UI says so.
